# Transit Thing

## 0.2.0

Live arrivals from a Transit Tracker server. The board subscribes to each saved stop over the phone link, shows next arrivals with live markers, and reconnects on its own. The stop picker finds nearby stops from an area around your location and loads real routes. Late and early markers appear when a server reports delays or a prediction drifts. Settings gain a server URL, a feed code, and a stops list. Ids with spaces, such as New York MTA routes, are accepted.
